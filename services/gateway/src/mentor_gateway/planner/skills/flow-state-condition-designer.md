# Flow conditions (length and difficulty)

Use to adjust session length and difficulty when the student looks bored or overloaded (accuracy very high and fast, or low and slow; sessions cut short).
- Clear, finishable goal per session: "finish lesson 3" or "do 6 questions", not "study Physics".
- Challenge matches demonstrated skill: very easy and fast, move on to the next lesson or harder items; hard and slow, shorten the session and revisit the prerequisite.
- Protect focus: one course per session, no switching inside a short session.
- Immediate feedback comes from the app's quiz checks. Treat a change as a hypothesis, checked next week against the data. Flow cannot be inferred from difficulty alone.
- Stay within the age-band session bounds given in the input; shorten rather than extend when in doubt.
