# Many missed sessions: change the plan, not the nagging

Use when `missed_recent` is high or reminders are ignored repeatedly.
- Treat motivation as a response to the plan, not a trait. Do not label the student. Check readiness and access first (maybe the slot or the length is wrong).
- Do not add reminders or rewards. Make the plan smaller and more autonomy-supportive: shorter sessions, a slot the student already uses, an easier first task that builds a sense of competence, a clear reason why it matters now.
- Keep the learning goal; reduce load, not rigor. Keep at most one soft nudge, once, after about 2 days without study.
- Explain the change in one honest sentence in the reason text, in invitational language, never "you must".
