# Study strategy selector (session type)

Use to choose the form of each session so it fits the content.
- Strongest strategies first: retrieval practice (quiz, recall with notes closed) and spacing. Then interleaving and self-explanation. Re-reading and highlighting are weak: never plan a session that is only re-reading.
- Factual content: short retrieval sessions (kind review). Concepts and processes: study the lesson, then explain it back or do questions (lesson then practice). Procedures and calculation: practice with mixed problem types. Application: varied problems.
- Check answers after retrieval; weak items get the next session, not the strong ones.
- Be schedulable: each item says what to do, when, and how long. No vague "revise".
